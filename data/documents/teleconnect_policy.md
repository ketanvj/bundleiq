# TRAI Compliance and Customer Rights -- TeleConnect India

## What is TRAI?

TRAI (Telecom Regulatory Authority of India) is the independent regulatory body that oversees India's telecommunications sector. TRAI sets tariff regulations, quality of service standards, and consumer protection guidelines that all telecom operators -- including TeleConnect India -- must follow.

TRAI's mandate includes ensuring that customers receive clear, accurate information about plans and charges, and that operators do not engage in unfair or deceptive practices.

---

## Your Rights as a TeleConnect Customer

Under TRAI regulations, you have the following rights:

**Right to clear tariff information:** TeleConnect India must make all plan prices, validity periods, and data allowances publicly available. You should never be surprised by a charge you were not told about in advance. If you are, raise a complaint (see below).

**Right to number portability:** You have the right to keep your mobile number when switching from one telecom operator to another. This is called Mobile Number Portability (MNP). Operators cannot prevent you from porting your number.

**Right to complaint redressal:** If TeleConnect India does not resolve your complaint within 30 days, you can escalate to TRAI's Consumer Affairs and Quality of Service (CA&QoS) division.

**Right to do-not-disturb registration:** You can register to stop receiving unsolicited commercial communications (promotional calls and SMS) from any entity.

## Mobile Number Portability (MNP) -- Porting Your Number

**Porting out from TeleConnect India (to another operator):**
1. Ensure your TeleConnect account is active and has no outstanding dues
2. SMS PORT [space] [your 10-digit mobile number] to 1900 (free SMS, available 24x7)
3. You will receive a Unique Porting Code (UPC) -- valid for 4 days
4. Submit the UPC to your new operator along with your ID proof
5. The porting process takes up to **7 working days** from the date of UPC submission
6. Your service continues on TeleConnect until the porting is complete -- there is no gap in service

**Note:** If you are on a bundle contract, porting your mobile number out may constitute an early exit and trigger the early exit fee. Contact TeleConnect support at 198 before initiating a port-out if you are in a contract period.

**Porting in to TeleConnect India:**
Visit any TeleConnect store with your existing SIM, ID proof, and the UPC from your current operator (obtained by SMSing PORT [number] to 1900 while on your current network). TeleConnect will process the port-in.

## Coverage and Network

TeleConnect India provides nationwide 4G coverage and expanding 5G coverage across major cities. Coverage at a specific location depends on the local network infrastructure.

**What TeleConnect cannot guarantee:**
- 5G coverage at a specific address or location -- 5G rollout is ongoing and coverage maps change
- Indoor coverage in all buildings (signal strength indoors depends on building materials and local network density)
- Specific speeds at all times (speeds vary based on network load and signal strength)

For current network coverage in your area, use the TeleConnect coverage checker at teleconnect.in/coverage or call 198.

For live network outage or maintenance information, check teleconnect.in/network-status or call 198. BundleIQ does not have access to real-time network status.

## Do-Not-Disturb (DND) Registration

To stop receiving unsolicited promotional calls and SMS from commercial entities:

1. Send **START DND** to **1909** (free SMS, available 24x7)
2. You will receive a confirmation message within 24 hours
3. Full DND activation takes up to 7 working days

After DND registration, you will still receive transactional messages (OTPs, bill alerts, service notifications) from TeleConnect India and other organisations you have a relationship with. DND blocks only unsolicited promotional communications.

## Complaint Process

**Step 1 -- Contact TeleConnect India:**
- Call 198 (24x7 helpline, toll-free from any TeleConnect number)
- Email: support@teleconnect.in
- Visit any TeleConnect store
- Use the TeleConnect app (Help > Raise a Complaint)

You will receive a complaint reference number. TeleConnect India must resolve your complaint within **30 days**.

**Step 2 -- If unresolved after 30 days, escalate to TRAI:**
- Visit TRAI's consumer complaints portal at trai.gov.in
- You can also call TRAI's helpline or write to: TRAI, Mahanagar Doorsanchar Bhawan, JLN Marg, New Delhi 110002

TRAI provides a free complaint resolution service and can direct the operator to resolve legitimate grievances.

## Billing Transparency

TeleConnect India bills are available:
- In the TeleConnect app (My Bills section)
- Via email (registered email address receives a bill each month)
- Via SMS (a summary is sent to your registered number each billing cycle)

If you believe you have been incorrectly charged, raise a complaint within 90 days of the bill date. Charges older than 90 days cannot be disputed.

## Customer Data Privacy -- DPDP Act 2023

TeleConnect India complies with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

**What data BundleIQ collects:**
BundleIQ collects only the information you share in the conversation (plan preferences, usage patterns, location -- city level only). This is used to recommend suitable plans and bundles. BundleIQ does not access your account details, call records, data usage history, or payment information during a chat session.

**What TeleConnect stores:**
Your account data (name, contact, SIM details, recharge history, usage) is stored in TeleConnect systems. BundleIQ can help you understand plan features but cannot access or display your personal account data.

**Your rights as a data principal:**
Under the DPDP Act 2023:
- **Access:** Request a summary of personal data TeleConnect holds about you via the TeleConnect app or by visiting a store
- **Correction:** Request correction of incorrect contact or account details
- **Erasure:** Request deletion of personal data where not legally required to be retained
- **Grievance redressal:** Contact TeleConnect's Data Protection Officer at dpo@teleconnect.in

**Data sharing:**
TeleConnect shares data with TRAI as required by law, and with payment processors for recharge transactions. Customer data is not sold to third parties.
