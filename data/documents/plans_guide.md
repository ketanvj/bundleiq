# Mobile Plans Guide -- TeleConnect India

## Types of Mobile Plans

TeleConnect India offers three types of mobile plans:

**Daily data plans:** A fixed amount of high-speed data per day (e.g., 1GB/day, 2GB/day, 3GB/day) valid for 28 days. These are the most common prepaid plans. After the daily data allowance is exhausted, your speed is reduced (see Fair Usage Policy below) -- your SIM remains active and you can still browse.

**Data top-up plans:** Additional data that you can add to any active plan when your daily allowance runs out. Top-up data is shared across days, not refreshed daily. Top-up plans do not include calls -- they are a data supplement to a primary plan.

**International roaming add-on plans:** Adds international calling and limited data usage when travelling outside India. These must be activated before you travel. They run alongside your primary plan (which continues for India usage when you return).

---

## Fair Usage Policy (FUP)

After you exhaust your daily data allowance, your internet speed is reduced to **512 kbps** for the rest of that day. At midnight, your allowance resets and full speed resumes.

**Important:** Your internet connection is not cut off -- it just becomes slower. At 512 kbps, basic browsing, messaging, and low-resolution video calls remain usable.

The daily reset means there is no benefit to spreading usage across a day if you have already exhausted your allowance -- wait for midnight for the reset.

---

## Data Rollover

TeleConnect India daily data plans do **not** include data rollover. Unused daily data does not carry forward to the next day. Each day starts fresh with the full allocated amount.

If you find yourself consistently not using your full daily allowance, a plan with a smaller daily allowance at a lower price may be more economical. Ask BundleIQ to compare plans for your usage pattern.

## Checking Your Data Balance

You can check your remaining daily data balance at any time:
- **SMS:** Send BAL to 198 (free from any TeleConnect number)
- **TeleConnect app:** The home screen shows your remaining daily data and plan validity
- **USSD code:** Dial *123# and follow the on-screen prompts

## 5G vs 4G Plans

TeleConnect India offers both 4G and 5G plans. Here is what you need to know before choosing a 5G plan:

**Three things are required for 5G to work -- all three must be in place:**
1. A **5G-capable mobile handset** (check your phone's specifications)
2. A **5G SIM** (TeleConnect 5G SIMs are available at stores -- your current 4G SIM may need to be swapped)
3. **5G network coverage** in your area (coverage varies by location and is expanding)

If any one of the three is missing, your device will fall back to 4G. A 5G plan on a 4G handset will work but you will only get 4G speeds.

For current 5G coverage information, check the TeleConnect coverage checker at teleconnect.in or call 198.

## Plan Switching

**Upgrading (moving to a higher-value plan):** Takes effect immediately. Your new plan activates as soon as the recharge is processed.

**Downgrading (moving to a lower-value plan):** The change takes effect at the start of your next billing cycle -- your current plan runs until its validity expires.

**How to switch:** Via the TeleConnect app (My Plans > Change Plan), or at any TeleConnect store. Plan switching can also be initiated by calling 198.

---

## Sharing Data (Mobile Hotspot)

Sharing your mobile data with other devices via Wi-Fi hotspot is a feature of your **handset**, not your plan. All TeleConnect plans allow hotspot usage -- the data consumed by devices connected to your hotspot counts against your own daily allowance.

TeleConnect does not offer a dedicated family data-sharing feature at the plan level -- each SIM has its own plan and allowance.

---

## Plan Validity

Most daily data plans are valid for **28 days** (not one month). This means you recharge approximately 13 times a year, not 12. Plan for this when budgeting your monthly mobile spend.

The Unlimited 5G plan has a **56-day validity** -- you recharge approximately 6-7 times a year.
