# Frequently Asked Questions -- TeleConnect India (BundleIQ)

---

**How do I check my remaining data balance?**
Send BAL to 198 (free SMS) or open the TeleConnect app -- the home screen shows your remaining daily data and plan validity. You can also dial *123# and follow the prompts.

---

**Why have my internet speeds slowed down?**
Most likely you have exhausted your daily data allowance. After your daily limit is used up, speeds drop to 512 kbps for the rest of that day (Fair Usage Policy). Speed returns to full at midnight when your daily allowance resets. To avoid this, check your balance regularly and consider a data top-up if you need full speed for the rest of the day.

---

**How do I recharge my plan online?**
Open the TeleConnect app, tap Recharge, select your plan, and pay via UPI, debit card, credit card, or net banking. You can also recharge at teleconnect.in or at any TeleConnect store or authorised retailer.

---

**Is there a network outage in my area?**
BundleIQ does not have access to live network status or outage information. For real-time outage updates, check teleconnect.in/network-status or call TeleConnect support at 198.

---

**How do I port my number in to TeleConnect?**
1. On your current network, SMS PORT [your 10-digit number] to 1900 to get a Unique Porting Code (UPC)
2. Visit any TeleConnect store with your UPC, ID proof, and existing SIM
3. TeleConnect will process the port-in -- it takes up to 7 working days
4. Your service on the current network continues until the port is complete

---

**My number is showing as unreachable -- what should I do?**
Try these steps in order:
1. Restart your phone
2. Remove the SIM, check it is properly seated, and reinsert it
3. Check that mobile data and calls are not blocked in your phone's settings (Settings > SIM > ensure the TeleConnect SIM is selected as the active SIM)
4. Check that you are not in a very low signal area (basements, concrete-heavy buildings)
5. If the problem persists after all of the above, call TeleConnect support at 198 for a SIM-level check

---

**Incoming calls are not working but I can make outgoing calls -- what is wrong?**
This usually indicates a network routing issue rather than a SIM problem. Try the same steps as above (restart, reseat SIM, check settings). If incoming calls still fail after restarting, call 198 and report the issue with your mobile number -- the support team can check the network-side configuration.

---

**How do I activate international roaming?**
Purchase the International Roaming Add-on plan before you travel (via the app or at a store). International roaming must be activated at least 2 hours before your departure. While abroad, enable data roaming in your phone settings. For calls, your standard roaming rates apply per the add-on plan.

---

**What is the difference between 4G and 5G plans?**
5G plans use the 5G network where available, offering higher speeds and lower latency. 4G plans use the 4G network everywhere TeleConnect has coverage. To use a 5G plan at 5G speeds, you need a 5G-capable handset, a 5G SIM, and to be in an area with 5G coverage. If any of these three is missing, a 5G plan works at 4G speeds. BundleIQ can show you 5G and 4G plan options and pricing.

---

**What is a bundle and is it worth it?**
A bundle combines your mobile plan and broadband plan (and optionally a device) into a single package at a combined price lower than buying each separately. Bundles also have 12-month contracts. Whether it is worth it depends on whether you need both mobile and broadband from the same provider and are comfortable with a 12-month commitment. Ask BundleIQ to compare the bundle price versus individual plan prices for your specific needs.

---

**How do I get my monthly invoice or bill?**
Your bill is sent to your registered email address each month and is also available in the TeleConnect app under My Bills. If you have not received a bill or need a duplicate, contact 198 or visit a store with your registered mobile number.

---

**How do I raise a complaint?**
Call 198 (24x7, toll-free from any TeleConnect number), email support@teleconnect.in, or use Help > Raise a Complaint in the TeleConnect app. You will receive a complaint reference number. TeleConnect must resolve complaints within 30 days. If unresolved after 30 days, escalate to TRAI at trai.gov.in.

---

**What is BundleIQ and what can it not do?**
BundleIQ is TeleConnect India's AI assistant for plan selection, broadband information, bundle comparisons, and general service queries. It can show you plan options, explain features, calculate bundle savings, and answer common questions.

BundleIQ cannot: check your live account details or balance (use the app or 198), check live network status or outages (check teleconnect.in/network-status), make plan changes on your account (use the app or 198), access your billing history, or process payments. For account-specific actions, always use the TeleConnect app or call 198.

---

**Is there a difference between MiFi and a router?**
A MiFi (TeleConnect 4G MiFi) is a portable battery-powered device that shares your mobile SIM's data as a Wi-Fi hotspot. It is useful when you are away from home. A Wi-Fi router (TeleConnect 5G WiFi Router) connects to a fixed broadband line via ethernet and provides in-home Wi-Fi. They serve different use cases -- BundleIQ can help you understand which is right for your situation.

---

**Can I use my TeleConnect SIM in any phone?**
TeleConnect SIMs work in any unlocked phone that supports the relevant network bands. Most modern smartphones support TeleConnect's 4G bands. For 5G, your phone must also support TeleConnect's 5G bands (check your phone's specifications). iPhones and Android phones both work with TeleConnect SIMs. Phones purchased from another Indian operator may be locked to that operator's network -- you would need to unlock them first.

---

**What happens after my plan's 28-day validity expires?**
If you do not recharge within 72 hours of plan expiry, incoming services (calls and SMS) may be suspended. Outgoing calls and data will be suspended immediately at expiry. Recharge to restore service. If the SIM remains inactive for 90 days, it may be deactivated and the number may be recycled. Set a reminder to recharge before your plan expires.

---

**Does TeleConnect offer family plans?**
TeleConnect India does not currently offer a family plan that shares a single data pool across multiple SIMs. Each SIM needs its own plan. However, you can purchase multiple plans simultaneously in the app or at a store. Ask BundleIQ if there are any household bundle options that may suit a family.

---

**What are TeleConnect's customer care hours?**
198 is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including public holidays. Store hours vary by location -- check teleconnect.in/stores for your nearest store and its hours.

---

**How do I set up a DND (Do Not Disturb) to stop promotional calls?**
Send START DND to 1909 (free SMS). DND registration takes up to 7 working days to take effect. After registration, you will stop receiving unsolicited promotional calls and SMS. You will still receive transactional messages (bills, OTPs, service updates) from TeleConnect India.

---

**Can I have multiple TeleConnect numbers?**
Yes. You can have multiple TeleConnect SIM cards under your name. Each SIM is managed as a separate connection with its own plan and recharge cycle. If you want to manage them together, the TeleConnect app allows you to add multiple numbers under one login for convenience.

---

**What is the difference between a prepaid and postpaid plan?**
TeleConnect offers prepaid plans, which require a recharge before use -- your balance is deducted as you use data and calls. Postpaid plans bill you at the end of each month based on your plan and usage. BundleIQ currently assists with prepaid plan selection. For postpaid plan enquiries, visit a TeleConnect store or call 198.

---

**How do I check my current bill or recharge amount?**
For prepaid connections, your current balance and data remaining are shown in the TeleConnect app or by sending BAL to 198. For postpaid, your monthly bill is available in the TeleConnect app under "My Bills." BundleIQ cannot access your account or display your bill -- use the app or call 198 for account-specific queries.

---

**Is TeleConnect available in my city?**
TeleConnect operates across major Indian cities and towns. For specific coverage in your area, check the TeleConnect coverage map on the official website or call 198. BundleIQ cannot confirm live coverage availability -- coverage depends on network rollout in your specific location.

---

**How do I cancel my broadband connection?**
To cancel your TeleConnect broadband connection, visit any TeleConnect store or call 198. A minimum notice period applies (typically 30 days for monthly plans). A technician will be scheduled to collect the ONU modem and other TeleConnect-provided equipment. Once equipment is returned and your account is settled, the connection will be closed. BundleIQ cannot process cancellations -- this must be done through a store or customer care.
