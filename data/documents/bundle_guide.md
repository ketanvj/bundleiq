# Bundle Guide -- TeleConnect India

## What is a Bundle?

A TeleConnect bundle combines multiple products -- typically a mobile plan, a broadband plan, and optionally a device -- into a single package at a combined price that is lower than buying each component separately.

Bundles are designed for households or individuals who want both mobile and broadband from a single provider, with one bill, one customer care number, and a combined saving.

---

## How Bundle Savings Work

When you buy a bundle, you pay a **bundle price** that is less than the sum of the individual component prices. The difference is your saving.

**Example (illustrative -- for current pricing, ask BundleIQ):**
- Mobile Plan A sold individually: Rs. X
- Broadband Plan B sold individually: Rs. Y
- Bundle (Mobile A + Broadband B): Rs. Z, where Z < X + Y
- Saving = (X + Y) - Z

The exact saving for each bundle is available from BundleIQ. Savings vary by bundle and change with promotional periods.

## Bundle Contract Terms

TeleConnect bundles require a **minimum 12-month commitment**. This is because the bundle discount is part of a value exchange: TeleConnect offers a lower combined price in return for a commitment period.

**Early exit fee:** If you cancel a bundle before the 12-month period ends, an early exit fee applies. The fee is proportional to the remaining contract period. The specific amount is stated in your bundle agreement at the time of sign-up.

After the 12-month period, the bundle continues on a month-to-month basis with no exit fee. You can cancel with 30 days' notice.

---

## Changing a Component After Bundling

If you want to change one component of your bundle (for example, upgrade to a faster broadband plan while keeping the same mobile plan), contact TeleConnect customer support at 198.

**Note:** Changing a component may affect the bundle pricing. In some cases, the new combination is available as a different bundle with different pricing. In other cases, you may need to convert to individual plans if the new combination is not available as a bundle. TeleConnect support will advise on your options.

---

## Bundle Activation Timeline

**Mobile SIM activation:** Same day (or within a few hours of the SIM being inserted and activated)

**Broadband installation:** Within 3 working days of the order, following the standard broadband installation process (see Broadband Guide)

Your first bill for the bundle will cover the partial month from activation to the end of the billing cycle, plus the first full month in advance.

---

## Device as Part of a Bundle

Some bundles include a device (TeleConnect 5G WiFi Router or TeleConnect 4G MiFi). If a device is included:
- The device is delivered to your address within 3 working days, or you can collect it from a TeleConnect store
- The device belongs to you (it is not rented) -- you keep it even if you cancel the bundle after the contract period
- Returning the device does not reduce the early exit fee if you cancel within the 12-month period
